WooCommerce Combo Packs

Complete guide to creating and managing product bundles with reservation capabilities

Introduction

Welcome to WooCommerce Combo Packs - a powerful plugin that enables you to create bundled product packages with advanced reservation and payment features. Perfect for educational institutions, businesses, and retailers who need to sell product combinations as single units.

What are Combo Products?

Combo products are bundled packages that contain multiple individual products sold together as a single unit. For example, a "School Starter Pack" might include notebooks, pens, erasers, and a ruler all bundled together at a special price.

Perfect for: School supplies, office kits, gift bundles, starter packages, seasonal collections, and any scenario where you want to sell multiple related products together.

Key Benefits

  • Increased Sales: Encourage customers to buy more items together
  • Simplified Shopping: Customers can purchase complete solutions in one click
  • Inventory Management: Track individual items within bundles
  • Flexible Payments: Allow customers to reserve items with partial payments
  • Professional Presentation: Showcase bundles attractively on your store

Who Should Use This Plugin?

Retail Stores

Bundle related products together to increase average order value and provide customer convenience.

B2B Suppliers

Create professional packages for businesses with payment terms and reservation capabilities.

Gift Shops

Curate themed gift bundles for holidays, special occasions, and corporate gifts.

Getting Started

Prerequisites

Requirements:
  • WordPress 5.0 or higher
  • WooCommerce 4.0 or higher
  • PHP 7.4 or higher
  • Active WooCommerce store with products

Installation Steps

Upload Plugin Files

Upload the plugin files to your WordPress site's /wp-content/plugins/ directory, or install directly through the WordPress admin panel.

Activate the Plugin

Navigate to Plugins > Installed Plugins and activate "WooCommerce Combo Packs for Products".

Verify WooCommerce

Ensure WooCommerce is installed and active. The plugin will display a notice if WooCommerce is missing.

Configure Settings

Go to WooCommerce > Settings > Products > Combo Packs to configure reservation and other settings.

Create Your First Combo

Navigate to Products > Add New and select "Combo Product" as the product type to start creating bundles.

Quick Start Tip: Start with a simple 2-3 item bundle to familiarize yourself with the interface before creating complex packages.

Creating Combo Products

Step-by-Step Guide

Start a New Product

Go to Products > Add New in your WordPress admin. Enter a product title and description for your combo package.

Select Combo Product Type

In the Product Data section, change the product type from "Simple Product" to "Combo Product".

Access Combo Product Tab

Click on the "Combo Product" tab that appears in the product data section.

Add Products to Bundle

Click "Add Product" and select products from your existing inventory. Set the quantity for each product in the bundle.

Configure Product Details

Set product images, categories, tags, and other standard WooCommerce product settings as needed.

Publish Your Combo

Click "Publish" to make your combo product available to customers. The price will be automatically calculated.

Reservation System

The reservation system allows customers to secure high-value combo products with partial payments, making expensive items more accessible while improving your cash flow.

How Reservations Work

Customer Selects Reservation

On eligible products, customers can choose to reserve the item with a deposit payment instead of paying the full amount.

Deposit Payment

Customer pays the configured percentage (e.g., 50%) as a deposit through their preferred payment method.

Order Status Management

Order is placed on "On Hold" status until the balance is paid, ensuring proper inventory tracking.

Balance Payment Collection

Admin can generate payment links for customers to pay the remaining balance when ready.

Order Completion

Once balance is paid, order automatically moves to "Completed" status and is ready for fulfillment.

Reservation Modes

Product Page Mode

  • Reservation option appears on individual product pages
  • Customers can choose to reserve each product independently
  • Best for: High-value individual items
  • Minimum threshold: Products over $100.00

Checkout Page Mode

  • Reservation option appears during checkout
  • Applies to all eligible items in the cart
  • Best for: Large orders or bulk purchases
  • Minimum threshold: Cart total over $100.00

Eligibility Configuration

Criteria Description Use Case
All Combo Products Every combo product allows reservations Simple setup, all bundles reservable
Specific Categories Only products in selected categories Seasonal items, premium collections
Custom Meta Value Products with specific custom field values Advanced control, custom business rules
Important: Reservation features only appear for combo products that meet the minimum price threshold and eligibility criteria you configure.

Managing Orders

The plugin provides comprehensive tools for managing combo product orders, especially those with reservations and balance payments.

Order Types

Regular Combo Orders

  • Full payment made at checkout
  • Standard WooCommerce order processing
  • Individual items tracked in order details
  • Ready for immediate fulfillment

Reservation Orders

  • Partial payment (deposit) made at checkout
  • Order status: "On Hold" until balance paid
  • Special metadata tracks payment status
  • Balance payment required before completion

Balance Payment Orders

  • Secondary orders created for balance collection
  • Linked to original reservation order
  • Virtual product representing balance due
  • Automatic completion triggers original order

Order Status Flow

Regular Combo Order Flow: Pending Payment → Processing → Completed Reservation Order Flow: Pending Payment → On Hold → Completed ↓ Balance Payment Link ↓ Balance Order Created ↓ Balance Payment Completed ↓ Original Order → Completed

Troubleshooting

Common Issues and Solutions

Plugin Installation Issues

Issue: Plugin shows "WooCommerce required" message
Solution: Ensure WooCommerce is installed and activated before activating the Combo Packs plugin.
Issue: Combo Product type not appearing
Solution: Deactivate and reactivate the plugin, then clear any caching plugins you may have.

Product Creation Issues

Problem Cause Solution
Can't add products to bundle JavaScript conflicts Check browser console, disable conflicting plugins
Price not calculating Product price issues Ensure all bundled products have valid prices
Products not saving Form validation errors Check all required fields are completed
Images not displaying Theme compatibility Check theme's single product template

Reservation System Issues

Issue: Reservation checkbox not showing
Solution: Check that:
  • Reservation system is enabled in settings
  • Product meets minimum price threshold ($100)
  • Product matches eligibility criteria
  • Product type is set to "Combo Product"
Issue: Payment gateway skipped during reservation
Solution: This was a known issue in earlier versions. Ensure you're using the latest version with the reservation payment fix.

Balance Payment Issues

Issue: "Cannot be paid for" error on balance payment
Solution: This occurs when balance orders aren't created properly. Update to the latest version which includes the balance payment fix.
Issue: Payment links not working
Solution: Check that:
  • Links haven't expired (24-hour limit)
  • Balance hasn't already been paid
  • Original order still exists
  • WordPress permalinks are set up correctly

Plugin Conflicts

Known Incompatibilities

  • Aggressive Caching: Some caching plugins may interfere with AJAX functionality
  • Theme Overrides: Themes that heavily modify WooCommerce templates
  • Other Bundle Plugins: Conflicts with other product bundling solutions
  • Custom Checkout Fields: Plugins that modify checkout process

Conflict Resolution

Identify the Conflict

Deactivate all other plugins except WooCommerce and Combo Packs to see if the issue resolves.

Test One by One

Reactivate plugins one at a time to identify which one causes the conflict.

Check Theme Compatibility

Switch to a default WordPress theme (like Twenty Twenty-Four) to test if the issue is theme-related.

Contact Support

If conflicts persist, contact support with details about the conflicting plugin or theme.

Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Q: Can I include variable products in combo bundles?

A: Yes, the plugin supports variable products within bundles. Customers can select variations during the add-to-cart process.

Q: How are bundle prices calculated?

A: Bundle prices are automatically calculated by multiplying each product's price by its quantity in the bundle, then summing all items. No manual price entry is required.

Q: Can I offer discounts on combo products?

A: Currently, the plugin uses automatic pricing based on individual product prices. For discounted bundles, you would need to adjust the individual product prices or use WooCommerce coupons.

Q: Do combo products affect inventory?

A: Yes, when a combo product is purchased, the inventory of each individual product within the bundle is properly decremented based on the quantities specified.

Reservation Questions

Q: What happens if a customer doesn't pay the balance?

A: The order remains in "On Hold" status and inventory is reserved. You can set up automated reminders or manually follow up with customers. You may also choose to cancel unpaid reservations after a certain period.

Q: Can I change the deposit percentage per product?

A: The current version uses a global deposit percentage setting. Per-product percentages are planned for future releases.

Q: How long are payment links valid?

A: Payment links expire after 24 hours for security reasons. You can generate new links if needed from the order admin page.

Q: Can customers pay the balance in multiple installments?

A: Currently, the system supports one deposit and one balance payment. Multiple installment support is planned for future versions.

Technical Questions

Q: Is the plugin compatible with [specific theme/plugin]?

A: The plugin is built to WooCommerce standards and should work with most well-coded themes and plugins. If you encounter issues, please test with a default theme and contact support.

Q: Can I customize the combo product display?

A: Yes, the plugin uses WooCommerce template structure. You can override templates in your theme following WooCommerce guidelines.

Q: Does the plugin work with WooCommerce subscriptions?

A: Combo products are designed for one-time purchases. Subscription compatibility is not currently supported.

Q: Can I export combo product data?

A: Combo products appear in standard WooCommerce exports. The individual items within bundles are tracked in order line items.

Business Questions

Q: How do I handle returns for combo products?

A: Handle returns according to your store policy. Since individual items are tracked, you can process partial returns if needed. The plugin doesn't enforce specific return policies.

Q: Can I create bundles from products in different categories?

A: Yes, you can include products from any categories in your bundles. The plugin doesn't restrict cross-category bundling.

Q: How do taxes work with combo products?

A: Taxes are calculated based on individual product tax settings within the bundle. The plugin respects WooCommerce tax configuration.

Q: Can I offer combo products to specific customer groups?

A: Use WooCommerce's built-in catalog visibility settings or role-based access plugins to control who can see combo products.
